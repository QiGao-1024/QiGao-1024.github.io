I listen to music, and I make music. My favourite artists include Lady Gaga (Artpop, Harlequin), Faye Wong (Fuzao) and Sufjan Stevens (Carrie & Lowell). I also love music in film, especially the Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon soundtrack.

I am a PhD student at Imperial College London, working on transcranial ultrasound imaging with full-waveform inversion. For the idea in full, please read this work.

I also study how ultrasound drives microbubble contrast agents and the nonlinear dynamics that follow. Ultrasound contrast agents hold great potential for clinical applications.

The goal is to bring these together. Non-invasive, radiation-free and efficient, sound can image a lesion, modulate neural activity and deliver a drug, quickly and safely.
